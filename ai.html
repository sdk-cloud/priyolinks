How to choose

Start with the tool that matches your task, not just the biggest model name.

For everyday questions: ChatGPT, Duck.ai or Gemini. For research: Perplexity. For AI images: Perchance. For browser-local experiments: gizza.ai. If a site asks you to log in unexpectedly, do not try to bypass its sign-in rules.
